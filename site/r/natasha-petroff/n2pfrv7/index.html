About this home

An exclusive Pre Market opportunity in The Crescent community of the coveted New Albany Country Club. This one-of-a-kind residence offers 5 bedrooms, 5 full baths, 2 half baths, and over 7,800 square feet of luxury living with breathtaking golf course views, lush architectural details, and thoughtful design.

A grand foyer opens to a den with French doors and custom built-ins, plus a formal dining room. The living room features a fireplace, custom built-ins, and large windows. The gourmet kitchen includes stone counters, custom backsplash, a 6-burner Bertazoni cooktop, double ovens, KitchenAid refrigeration, and Restoration Hardware fixtures. A spacious family room with fireplace and laundry complete the main level.

The owner suite is on the main level with plush carpeting, a fireplace, walk-in closet, and a remodeled spa bath with heated floors, dual vanities, and freestanding soaker tub. Upstairs includes an incredible carriage suite. The finished lower level adds a wet bar, large recreation room, guest room, and full bath. Outdoors: a private brick patio and professional landscaping.
