About Natasha

Hello! I am Natasha Petroff. I pride myself on providing exceptional customer service, attentive communication, and focus on nuanced details. My goal is to blend my passion for people with the high professional standards of the Thomas Riddle team. Whether I am representing first-time home buyers or seasoned investors, I strive to make the real estate transaction as smooth and as stress-free as possible — centrally focused on achieving my client’s goals. The greatest compliment is personal referrals and repeat clients. Fortunately, I have frequently been blessed by both.

Outside of real estate, my focus is my family. Although my husband and I are both originally from the eastside suburbs of Cleveland, we both moved to Columbus to attend grad school at The Ohio State University. In 2010 we moved to New Albany to raise our family. We have three daughters, all of whom attend New Albany Plain Local schools. While most of our free time is spent on the sidelines coaching and cheering our daughters in travel basketball, lacrosse, swim & dive, and golf, when we do get an occasional moment, we very much enjoy spending time outdoors with friends and family playing various racquet sports.
