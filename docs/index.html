How private previews work

Simple, calm, and controlled—so you never feel like inventory on a public billboard.

1. You work with a realtor

Your agent (buyer’s or listing) decides when a private preview is appropriate and who should see it.

2. You receive an invite link

That link is personal and branded to the realtor who sent it—not a public search result anyone can browse.

3. You review on your time

Photos, facts, and (when invited) richer map context—without exposing every homeowner’s details to the open web.
